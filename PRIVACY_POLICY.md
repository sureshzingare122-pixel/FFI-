# Privacy Policy — FFI Trainer Pro

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026  
**Application:** FFI Trainer Pro (`com.ffitrainerpro.app`, Version 1.0.0)

## Offline Privacy Declaration

FFI Trainer Pro is an offline training simulator. It does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It does not modify or interact with Free Fire India.

## Data Storage & Permissions

- **100% Local Storage:** Training sessions, movement profiles (`RUSH`, `DEFENSIVE`, `SNIPER`), sensitivity calibration results, and app preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android Room (SQLite) and Jetpack DataStore.
- **No Network Access:** FFI Trainer Pro does not request `INTERNET` permission and performs zero network requests, analytics telemetry uploads, or advertising tracking.
- **No Invasive Permissions:** The application does not request `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, Accessibility Services, broad storage permissions, location, camera, or contacts.
- **User-Initiated Exports:** CSV analytics exports and text summaries are generated locally inside the app's private cache directory and shared only when you explicitly tap Export or Share via the Android system share sheet.
- **Data Deletion:** You may clear individual sessions or all training history at any time from the **STATISTICS** screen, or reset preferences from **SETTINGS**.
